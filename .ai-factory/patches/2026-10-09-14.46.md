# Technique lifecycle diffusion metrics used the wrong counts

**Date:** 2026-10-09 14:46
**Files:** src/analysis/technique_lifecycle.py, src/analysis/technique_lifecycle_metrics.py, src/analysis/specifications.py, docs/architecture.md, src/experiments/__init__.py, tests/unit/test_technique_lifecycle_metrics.py
**Severity:** medium

## Problem

`technique_lifecycle_diffusion@1` reported `mean_location_spread` as the mean number of lineage roots and `mean_hop` as the mean of `known_by_n`. `technique_lifecycle_state@1` had a mean holder count and no `known_by_n` histogram. The cultural seam row in `docs/architecture.md` omitted `runner-config-v37`. Experiment AR exports in `src/experiments/__init__.py` were unsorted.

## Root Cause

The snapshot carried lineage root ids and `known_by_n`, and the metric functions treated those fields as location spread and hop. Location counts and living-holder hops were computed inside the classifier and then discarded. The histogram was never specified as a value key, so tests that only checked `discovered_count` stayed green.

## Solution

Each snapshot now stores `location_count` (places other than `location:unknown`), `living_hop_sum`, and `living_hop_count` (latest living hop per holder). Diffusion averages those fields. State documents add `known_by_n_histogram` as `n:row_count` pairs. The seam row names `TechniqueLifecycleSpec` on v37 and Experiment AR. Exports were sorted with ruff.

## Prevention

When a metric family names a quantity, store that quantity on the snapshot and assert it in a known-answer test. Do not substitute a nearby count such as lineage roots or `known_by_n`.
