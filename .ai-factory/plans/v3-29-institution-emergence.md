## Goal

Let researchers ask — after a run, from detached evidence only — whether institution-like structures have emerged: patterns of persistent recognized role, repeated procedure, social expectation, legitimacy belief, succession, and cross-generational continuity. The analytics must separate genuinely persistent institution-like structure from temporary coordination (a one-off collective project, a brief gathering) and must never hard-code institution categories — no government, religion, court, police, corporation, or political party exists anywhere in the system. Independently, agents may (opt-in, default off) form their own owner-scoped subjective concepts of institutions from their own evidence, the same way they already form group, norm, and convention beliefs. Everything detected is an analytical candidate or a private belief; nothing becomes agent-visible world truth.

## Design Decisions

### Subjective institution-concept primitive (opt-in, owner-scoped)

- New module `src/agents/cognition/institution_concepts.py`, locked policy `institution-concepts.v1` (compile-time-locked constants; any deviation rejects with `unsupported_policy`). Model: `InstitutionConceptLedger` of `InstitutionConcept` items keyed by opaque sha256 concept ids (derived from owner id + sorted evidence digest list — RNG-free, no `hash()`).
- Each concept carries **closed pattern-strength channels** mirroring the six analytical patterns — `role_recognition`, `procedure_recurrence`, `social_expectation`, `legitimacy_belief`, `succession_observed`, `generational_continuity` — each a quantized float in [0, 1] with its own `InstitutionConceptEvidence` list (channel OBSERVED/COMMUNICATED/REMEMBERED, lineage ref to the owner's own memory/event tokens, tick). Evidence is drawn only from the owner's `Observation` + identity projection + the owner's existing ledgers (norms, conventions, groups, possession legitimacy); cognition never sees other agents' private state or `WorldEvent` stores.
- Lifecycle: `candidate` → `recognized` (promotion floor: ≥3 independent evidence items and ≥3 pattern channels above the locked threshold, mirroring norm/convention promotion) → `fading` → retired via locked decay. An `InstitutionConceptualization` enum (none/named_usual/named_custom, convention precedent) lets an owner attach a self-contained opaque custom label backed by COMMUNICATED custom evidence; there is no world dictionary, no shared label authority, and no `TerminologyLedger`/`semantic_naming_mode` dependency.
- Communication: `institution_communicate_utterance()` stamps `Talk`/`Tell` with the same relation-recipe mechanism as norms and may emit a trust request; transmitted concepts reinforce the receiver's own evidence (+delta locked) but never copy the sender's ledger rows. Two owners may hold contradictory concepts about the same regularity — that is expected and preserved.
- Update entry point `apply_institution_concept_update()` wired in `src/agents/cognition/loop.py` behind `CognitionInstitutionConceptMode` after the norm/convention/possession/group stages and before proposal construction; runtime commit in `agent_runtime.py`; checkpoint validation via `require_owner_institution_concepts` in `run_control.py` (translation precedent `run_control.py:725-730`); policy version carried by the locked `institution-concepts.v1` policy constant — no codec marker, no codec/replay bump (v3-27 precedent).

### Analytical institution detection (detached, read-only)

- New module `src/analysis/institution_emergence.py`. It owns the frozen lane row DTOs; `src/experiments/composition.py` harvests them from detached evidence (never the reverse import direction). An **institution candidate** is a content-derived opaque id over a bundle of six pattern lanes:
  - **role persistence** — recurrence of the same actor in function-bearing objective event roles (repository founder/maintainer, collective-project initiator/regular contributor, public-good maintainer, teaching/mentorship source) over a locked minimum tick span;
  - **procedure recurrence** — repeated closed action-sequence digests bound to the same location/day-phase (sequence of action-kind digests only, never content);
  - **social expectation** — active norm beliefs and sanction rows harvested from the new owner-ordered norm-ledger export through existing cultural-channel row shapes;
  - **legitimacy belief** — possession-legitimacy doctrines and sanction notes plus norm supporter/sanction distributions from the new owner-ordered possession/norm exports (subjective-derived rows, digests only);
  - **succession** — the same function resumed by a *different* actor after the prior actor's death event (objective lifecycle death + function-lane evidence);
  - **cross-generational continuity** — role/procedure evidence spanning lifecycle generation boundaries (kinship generations, mentorship teacher→mentee chains).
- Output DTOs: `InstitutionCandidateRow` (candidate_id, per-lane quantized strengths plus opaque evidence digests, lane coverage, actor-id digest list, tick range, generation span), `InstitutionEmergenceResult` (candidates, sub-threshold bundles, coverage, known_person_ids). Missing lane → `absent`, never zero.
- New metric family `institution_emergence@1`: candidates above the specification's locked pattern-bundle floor, quantized per `numerical.py`; determinism (seed-identical runs → identical candidate sets and ids) is arbiter-tested.

### Institutional persistence metrics

- New module `src/analysis/institutional_persistence.py`, family `institutional_persistence@1`. It consumes the analysis-owned lane/candidate rows and the detached temporal evidence. Computed over the detected candidates' full temporal evidence (not just end-state):
  - **lifetime distribution** and quantized survival curve of candidates over ticks (Kaplan–Meier-style bookkeeping over logical ticks, deterministic, no RNG);
  - **succession survival** — fraction of function lanes that continue past a principal actor's death, per lane;
  - **cross-generational continuity ratio** — share of candidate evidence attributable to more than one lifecycle generation;
  - **temporary-coordination vs persistent-institution classification** — each candidate is classified by locked thresholds (tick span, post-death survival, generation span, pattern-floor persistence); the classification is a reported value with explicit numerators/denominators, and sub-threshold bundles are reported as `temporary_coordination` with their own counts — never suppressed.
- `METRIC_FAMILY_COUNT` 115 → 117; one `MetricDocument` per family with availability/coverage/provenance, assembled behind the fail-closed `_safe` wrapper in `metric_service.py` through explicit `MetricComputationInputs` fields.

### Experiment BF (off the V1 regression gate)

- New `src/experiments/institutionalization_scenario.py` + catalog builder `experiment_bf_institutionalization` (id `institutionalization@1`): long-tick multi-generational world composing already-owned channels (lifecycle v26+, kinship, mentorship, collective projects, public goods, repositories, norms, conventions, exchange) with `institution_concept_mode=DETERMINISTIC` on `runner-config-v49`. Append `institutionalization@1` to `OFF_GATE_MATRIX_EXPERIMENT_IDS` (precedent: `regional_migration@1`, `multiple_societies@1`) and assert membership in the experiment test.
