## Compatibility contract

This plan owns the already-declared `V2CapabilityFlags.extended_self_model` field. It must satisfy the Downstream V2 plan contract in `docs/architecture.md`.

1. V1 invariants stay intact: `WorldEngine` authority, Observation trust, append-only history, subjective state outside the objective fold, LLM non-authority, no cross-agent copy, reproducible seeds.
2. `extended_self_model` stays default **off**. Off means the V1 `project_self_model` path and no identity influence. Other reserved flags stay unimplemented and still fail closed with `capability_unimplemented`. Enabling this owned flag must not raise that error.
3. `tests/unit/test_v1_regression_gate.py` stays green under flags-off and tracing-off. Flag-on behavior is a separate experiment and must not be added to that gate.
4. No runner schema bump. `extended_self_model` is already an exact key on `runner-config-v3+`. Default write stays `runner-config-v4`. Do not add identity keys to runner JSON.
5. No scripted identity classes and no friend/enemy/leader labels. Aspect kinds describe what a belief is about. They do not name what kind of person the agent is.
6. No LLM path in this plan. Identity claims are deterministic and evidence-cited. LLM prose never becomes a claim, role, or value label.
7. Experiments stay on `experiment-definition-v1`. Paired arms share seed, scenario, and stochastic identity. Experiment H stays off the V1 regression gate.
8. Optional cognition tracing stays outside the objective fold. This plan does not insert a `CognitiveLoop` ordinal and does not bump the cognition-trace schema or Alembic. Tracing on versus off must not change `exact_trajectory_hash` when identity mode is `PASSTHROUGH`.

## Goal

Extend the V1 self-model so an agent can form subjective, revisable beliefs about its abilities, weaknesses, recurring behavior, values inferred from its own choices, social roles, relationships, commitments, perceived status, reliability, risk tolerance, and competence. Those beliefs emerge from the owner's history. The same initial configuration plus different histories must yield different self-models. When the flag is on, the resulting self-model biases goal selection, planning, willingness to break a past commitment, interpretation of social feedback, learning, and reflection. Actions that conflict with an established self-belief produce cognitive dissonance: contradicting evidence and a lower willingness threshold next time, without a hard ban on the action.

## Design Decisions

- **Own the existing flag.** Add `extended_self_model` to `_V2_OWNED_CAPABILITY_FLAGS`. Do not add a new flag name. Mirror it with `CognitionIdentityMode` (`PASSTHROUGH` | `ENABLED`) on `CognitionLoopConfig`. Runner mapping: flag false → `PASSTHROUGH`; flag true → `ENABLED`. Unit tests may inject `ENABLED` without the runner.
- **One belief store.** Identity is not a second database and not a persisted `SelfModel` row. Formation emits ordinary `BeliefRevisionRequest` values. `BeliefRevisionService.revise` remains the only writer. The next prepare-stage `project_self_model` reads the updated head. `SemanticBeliefHistory` is the history.
- **Aspects are topics, not person classes.** Closed `IdentityAspect`: `ABILITY`, `WEAKNESS`, `RECURRING_BEHAVIOR`, `INFERRED_VALUE`, `SOCIAL_ROLE`, `RELATIONSHIP`, `COMMITMENT`, `PERCEIVED_STATUS`, `RELIABILITY`, `RISK_TOLERANCE`, `COMPETENCE`. Forbidden identity tokens, rejected at claim construction: `warrior`, `leader`, `trader`, `good_person`, `evil_person`, `goodperson`, `evilperson`, `friend`, `enemy`. No `TEXT` claim values under the identity policy.
- **Stable claim, derived rate.** Stored identity claims are `BOOL` `true` for the owner subject. Predicate grammar is `identity.<aspect>.<provenance>.<token>`. `aspect` and `provenance` are closed enum values. `token` is copied from evidence already in context: a command-kind code, a drive id, a relationship-dimension code, an owner goal id, or a counterpart agent id. Do not store a changing `NUMBER` or `AGENT` value. `merge_revision` keeps the prior claim when a later value differs and remaps the new support onto contradiction, so a moving rate cannot be the stored value. The view derives the rate as quantized `support_count / (support_count + contradiction_count)` from the head counts.
- **Structured view.** `IdentityBeliefView` carries `belief_id`, aspect, provenance kind, evidence token code, claim, confidence, derived rate, supporting memory ids, contradicting memory ids, derived stability, activation state, and an ordered revision summary (ordinal, tick, activation). Confidence and both evidence stances stay on the existing belief head and evidence bundle. Do not add Alembic columns for them.
- **Provenance lives in the predicate.** `BeliefRevisionService.revise` ignores `request.policy` and `request.activation_state`. It stamps `semantic-belief-formation` / `1` from the service policy. Provenance is the predicate segment: `observed_outcome`, `own_choice`, `social_feedback`, `relationship_evidence`, or `goal_outcome`. The projection parses those segments and fails closed on an unknown one. Do not assert that the stored `BeliefPolicyRef` starts with `identity.`. Each request still sets `policy` to `DEFAULT_BELIEF_FORMATION_POLICY.as_ref()` so the ignored field matches the writer reflection already uses.
- **Stability is derived.** Quantized unit interval from the revision chain only: ordinal depth, ticks since the last contradicting revision, and absolute confidence change from the previous revision. Constants live only on versioned `IdentityPolicy` (`policy_id="identity-projection"`, `version="1"`). Empty history is not a view. A first revision has low stability.
- **Activation follows the existing formation policy.** Do not change `DEFAULT_BELIEF_FORMATION_POLICY`. A new belief stays `CANDIDATE` until independent support is at least 2 and confidence is at least 0.4. `project_identity_state` includes `CANDIDATE` and `ACTIVE` at or above `IdentityPolicy.min_confidence`. It omits `RETIRED`. The view records the head activation state.
- **Evidence rule.** A revision is emitted only when at least one owner `MemoryId` already in the snapshot or current-tick traces can be cited. No citable memory yields reason code `no_memory_evidence` and no request. Identity appraisal does not read `SubjectiveDecisionRecord` or the reflection cursor. Decision-record ids never become belief evidence. Reflection remains the only writer of predicates copied from a cited `MemoryRelation`, including `repeated_action` and `repeated_help`. Identity never emits those predicates and never emits `GoalTransitionIntent` for `repeated_failure` or `prediction_error`.
- **Causal order.** Prepare projects yesterday's identity and may influence this tick. `CognitiveLoop.complete` appraises the chosen action only after memory update, sleep consolidation, and reflection, then drops any belief id those writers already revised. `finalize_pending` applies the rest after a successful `resolve_tick`. Dissonance therefore changes the next tick, not the command already selected.
- **Dissonance is contradiction, not a veto.** Closed `IdentityConflictCode` pairs a selected command kind with a commitment, inferred-value, or risk-tolerance view (`CANDIDATE` or `ACTIVE`). The action still proceeds. Contradicting evidence cites a `MemoryId` and keeps the same `BOOL` claim, so `merge_revision` accumulates contradiction instead of replacing the value. Physiological critical vetoes in `MultiCriteriaIntentionSelector` stay as they are.
- **Willingness is one pairwise vote.** Flag-on `_pairwise_compare` adds an identity vote from `violation_cost = quantize(commitment_confidence * stability * conflict_weight)`. High cost can lose the contest to another feasible future. It cannot drop the last feasible future or override `_critical_vetoes`. `CommandPlanner.plan` still compiles exactly one command from the winning future through `_compile_command` (`candidate_count=1`). It does not grow a second command list.
- **Social interpretation is a bounded multiplier.** Relationship and reliability views scale the unit-interval trust from `project_trust_inputs` for the cited counterpart. The factor stays inside `IdentityPolicy.social_scale_floor`..`social_scale_ceiling` (default 0.85–1.15). The product is clamped back to `[0, 1]` before `evaluate_communicated_testimony`. Missing views use factor `1`. This does not replace the speaker's claim, create a reverse relationship edge, or invent a role label.
- **Learning and reflection stay the writers they already are.** Identity does not re-revise belief ids present on the consolidation plan or the reflection plan. When both reflection mode and identity mode are enabled, dissonant memory ids are appended to `ReflectionContext.cited_memory_ids`, which `_select_memories` already prefers. Do not add a `self_model` field to `ReflectionContext`. Reflection still does not persist `SelfModel` and still does not enable `extended_self_model`.
- **V1 projection remains.** `DEFAULT_SELF_MODEL_POLICY` version `"1"` and `SelfRelevantBelief` stay. `SelfModel.identity` defaults to `None`. `PASSTHROUGH` leaves it `None` and keeps today's belief selection. `ENABLED` fills `IdentityState` from beliefs whose predicate uses the identity grammar, including `CANDIDATE` heads, and still keeps the V1 belief tuple for owner-referential beliefs.
- **Resume.** `AgentRuntimeCheckpoint` is an in-memory dataclass, not a JSON document. Add optional `identity_cursor` defaulting to `None`, and thread it through `export_runtime_checkpoint` and `restore_runtime_checkpoint` the same way as `reflection_cursor`. The cursor stores the last applied logical tick and the ordered operation ids applied on that tick. Same-process re-finalize also skips ids already in an in-memory applied set on `AgentRuntime`. Do not bump a checkpoint schema. No new subjective table.
- **Metrics stay analysis-only.** New family `identity_dynamics@1` consumes an identity audit report. It records aspect counts, stability bands, dissonance counts, and between-agent divergence. It does not store claim text. Live cognition never reads the report.

## Non-Goals

- Fixed classes or labels such as Warrior, Leader, Trader, GoodPerson, EvilPerson, friend, or enemy
- A persisted self-model document, personality trait vector, or second belief table
- Alembic migration for belief columns
- A new `CognitiveLoop` ordinal, a cognition-trace schema bump, or an Alembic revision
- A runner-config v7 or new capability-flag JSON keys
- LLM-authored identity, free-form self narrative, or prompt changes
- Enabling `advanced_social_inference`, `multi_hop_testimony_tracking`, or `predictive_world_model`
- Hard-forbidding an action because it conflicts with a self-belief
- Feeding analysis reports or truth specs back into cognition
- Rewriting V1 `project_self_model` selection when the flag is off
- Reading the decision journal or reflection cursor from identity appraisal, deliberation, imagination, or motivation
- Letting identity emit reflection's copied `MemoryRelation` predicates or turn `repeated_failure` / `prediction_error` into belief revisions
